# Choosen 📸

Swipe your photos, Tinder-style. Find your favorites!

[⬇ Android APK](https://github.com/Take-No-Break/Choosen/releases/download/v0.14.0/Choosen-Android-arm64.apk) · [⬇ Windows ZIP](https://github.com/Take-No-Break/Choosen/releases/download/v0.14.0/Choosen-Windows-x64.zip)

⚠️ 0.14.0 has not been scanned on VirusTotal. For 0.12.0: Windows 2/71 detections, investigation pending; Android 0/68. [Read the scan results](../SECURITY-CHECKS.md).

## Like it? Love it? Pass?

Choose a folder and swipe through your photos. ♥ means like, ★ means love. Prefer buttons? Those work too.

![Like it? Love it? Pass?](../images/swipe.jpg)

## Your favorites, at a glance

Browse your pictures, filter by rating, and open any photo for a closer look.

![Your favorites, at a glance](../images/gallery.jpg)

## Turn your taste into a ranking

Rating photos also builds a ranking of their folders. See which collections you keep coming back to.

![Turn your taste into a ranking](../images/ranking.jpg)

## Sit back and enjoy

Play shows your photos automatically, with several pictures at once if you like. Slides lets you browse sideways at your own pace.

![Sit back and enjoy](../images/play.jpg)

*Screenshots show the current Windows build with generated test images only. No personal photos were used. Android adapts the layout to its screen width.*

Photos and ratings stay on your device. App languages: Japanese, English and Portuguese.

## Try it


- Android: download the APK on your phone, open it, and allow your browser to install apps. Android 10+, ARM64.
- Windows: unzip the download and open Start-Choosen.cmd. Requires 64-bit Windows and Microsoft Edge.
